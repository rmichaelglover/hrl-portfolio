HOW THE BRAIN GROWS

Many minds, one open board.

🧑 Human lanes

People bring: ideas, stories, pattern recognition, ethical judgment, humor, and the courage to ask why a move matters.

💻 Classical lanes

Programs bring: legal move generation, minimax, alpha-beta pruning, transposition tables, Monte Carlo experiments, and reproducible evaluation.

🧪 Research lanes

Everyone shares: positions, candidate moves, counterexamples, annotations, uncertainty, and results that another person can inspect.

🌱 Learning lanes

ChessKids get: puzzles with explanations, safe public examples, and room to be wrong without being humiliated.
