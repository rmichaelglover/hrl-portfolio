Your premises

One statement per line. Terms are matched literally; use the same spelling in facts and rules.

What English does this compiler accept?

“Every TERM is TERM”, “Every TERM is not TERM”, “NAME is TERM”, and “NAME is not TERM”. Terms use English letters, spaces, or hyphens. A term such as “bachelor” is a predicate; a name such as “Manny” identifies an individual. This is a deliberately small fragment, not a compiler for unrestricted English.

The derivation

    These are consequences of supplied premises, not verified claims about people. Unsupported lines are excluded and clearly reported. Conflicts are displayed without deriving unrelated claims; this prototype uses forward rules, not the full classical calculus.
