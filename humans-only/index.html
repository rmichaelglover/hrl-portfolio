01

People belong. Abuse doesn’t.

You can be blunt, irreverent, angry, or wrong. Criticize ideas and conduct. No stalking, sustained targeting, threats, doxxing, hate directed at people for who they are, or coordinating harassment. “Just joking” does not cancel a boundary.

02

A room is a promise.

All-ages rooms keep language and subjects suitable for a broad audience. Ages 18–21 means eighteen through twenty-one, inclusive. The 18+ and 21+ rooms have their stated minimums. Adult rooms are not permission for harassment, sexual solicitation, or graphic material.

Age declarations are self-reported and renewed every thirty days. They are not identity or age verification. Accounts, rather than a browser checkbox, control which restricted messages the server returns.

03

Seven days. Then another chance.

A moderator can remove harmful content and pause an account’s commenting across forum and game chat for exactly seven days. The timer cannot be stacked while active. You can read, report, and appeal during the pause. There is no permanent-ban command. Further misconduct after returning can lead to another seven-day pause.

Reports request human review; they do not automatically punish someone. Moderation records include a reason. An accepted appeal can end a timeout early.

04

Bring your own humanity.

No automated accounts or automated posting. Pseudonyms are welcome. No private messages or file uploads in this first version. Don’t post personal information—yours or someone else’s. Moderators can review reports and reported content.

Passwords are hashed. Posts, account names, age declarations, reports, and moderation records live on the community server. Public all-ages posts are visible to everyone. Keep a private copy of your passphrase; there is no email recovery.
