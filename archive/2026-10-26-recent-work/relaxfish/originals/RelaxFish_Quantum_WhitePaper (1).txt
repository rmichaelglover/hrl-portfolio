Abstract
Traditional approaches to finite discrete adversarial spaces—such as standard chess game trees—rely heavily on
combinatorial alpha-beta minimax searches and unconstrained brute-force lookaheads. These architectures exhibit explosive
computational bottlenecks and unpredictable latency scaling when restricted to real-time edge computing nodes. Here we
introduce Relaxfish, a hybrid classical-quantum constraint satisfaction paradigm that replaces combinatorial tree evaluations
with continuous, non-linear relaxation labeling matrices running over a bounded Hilbert space. By formalizing tactical
structures as interactive role-assignment superpositions, the system converts sequence searches into a gradient-ascent
potential optimization. We demonstrate the practical utility of this multi-scale framework by analyzing state trajectories during
highly unconventional asymmetrical openings, verifying that the introduction of a non-zero semantic noise buffer element
successfully absorbs unmodeled environmental and adversarial friction. The system eliminates computational timeouts,
converging deterministically onto stable, globally optimal equilibria in O(K * N^2 * L^2) operations.

1. Introduction and Mathematical Formulation
The combinatorial state space of standard chess contains approximately 10^120 discrete layouts, rendering absolute
brute-force solutions or unguided tree updates computationally unfeasible at the edge. Traditional game theory attempts to
manage this complexity by applying linear pruning heuristics to evaluate positional branches sequentially. Relaxfish eliminates
the move-tree graph entirely by modeling the physical environment as a self-consistent network of interacting objects and
functional identities.
Let a configuration be defined within a complex Hilbert space H. For any active piece element i, its tactical profile is
represented as a normalized pure state vector, denoted by a linear combination of orthogonal basis vectors: |psi_i〉 = Σ c_{ik}
|φ_k〉, where the components represent key operational states (King-Shield, Infiltrator, Blockader, Open-File, and Null-Noise),
and c_ik marks the complex probability amplitudes. Adherence to the unitary probability simplex boundary is enforced via the
Born rule, ensuring that total systemic density satisfies the unit normalization perfectly.

2. Piece Algebra and Classical Machine Constraints
The primary matrix layer projects environmental properties as fixed, worldly vector templates, implementing an algebraic
composition law for active components. A Queen is defined as the perfect additive union of lateral and diagonal projection
fields: Queen = Rook + Bishop. Conversely, a King operates as a bounded Queen restricted to a local spatial radius of 1.
To manage physical obstructions without initiating depth loops, the framework codifies a non-linear deflection law: friendly
assets are treated as immutable, solid boundaries, reflecting incoming tracking signals cleanly. This incorporates the
fundamental rule of thumb: you cannot capture your own pieces. Friendly layouts thus cease to be passive space obstacles
and become active defensive armor shields, preventing adversarial penetration fields from breaching the inner coordinate
sectors.
