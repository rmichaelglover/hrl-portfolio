01 / OUR FOUNDING PRINCIPLE

Let it be written.
Let it be done.

Success means people remain safe and essential services keep working. The aim is stability, not domination.

We investigate systems to protect people, strengthen defenses, and make room for peace.

We test with authorization, protect private information, and report weaknesses responsibly.

Our words carry meaning. Our claims answer to evidence. We seek containment, recovery, and de-escalation—not preemptive strikes.

THE STALEMATE GAMBIT · FOUNDING CHARTER

THE LOWER CHAIR FIRST

Power is not permission.

Protect the victims. Stop the harm. Keep accountability. Let force end when its necessity ends.

Receive authority.

Act only under explicit, lawful authorization from someone entitled to grant it. Good intentions, technical access, confidence, or a feeling of divine permission do not establish that authority.

Respect its limits.

Keep to the named systems, permitted actions, purpose, and time window. Protect private information and essential services. Use the least harmful effective response; never turn containment into retaliation.

Know when to stop.

Stop when permission expires or is revoked, the protective need ends, or safety becomes uncertain. Preserve evidence, report the action, and restore normal service when safe.
