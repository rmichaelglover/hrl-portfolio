Relaxation labeling treats a chess position as a field of possibilities. Candidate moves begin with weights; compatible continuations support one another; conflicting lines lose weight. The output is a ranked conversation, not a claim of certainty.

One position, many intentions

Illustrative candidates after 1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6. Drag the iteration slider: the field settles as compatibility is applied repeatedly.

The update

xᵢ(t+1) = ΠΔ [ xᵢ(t) + η Σⱼ Cᵢⱼ xⱼ(t) ]

xᵢ is a candidate label, Cᵢⱼ is compatibility, η is the step size, and ΠΔ returns the weights to a valid simplex. “Noise” remains visible when memory is weak.

What this can say

It can rank remembered continuations and expose which motifs reinforce a line. It cannot replace legal move generation, tactical calculation, or human judgment. A stable label is a computational result under chosen compatibilities—not proof that the move wins.
