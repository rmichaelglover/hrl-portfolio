      What is this little world modeling?

      The Glims is Michael Emanuel Glover’s household-life simulation at Glover Machine Works. It brings his virtual Person framework into an interactive illustration. It uses modeled geometry and two eye origins 64 mm apart for visibility, collision-aware movement, short-lived visual observations, remembered objects, several competing goals, independent family replies, and separate practice states.

      Alex remains the playable person; Cypher and Rosy are independent simulated housemates, with small routines and their own availability. Mama is present in the room, and the household can paint and read together. Paintings and saves stay in your browser. The room and all meters are simulated. This is not live ROS telemetry or a retinal object-recognition system. The C++ Eyes, physiological models, HRL/GRRLE experiments, and real local sleep-study pipeline have their own executable implementations.
