Alex at home

Listening to Yesterday. Practicing guitar, voice, and lyric recall. Considering leftovers. Finding time for Papa, Mama, and longer-term goals.

Michael Emanuel Glover’s household scenario gives Alex simultaneous desires, a subjective map, and models of other people whose preferences remain their own.

Seeing is different from remembering

The existing Eyes process room light and retinal activity while movement updates both optical frames. A separate, explicitly supplied range-and-bearing frontend identifies household landmarks. Scalar rod signals do not yet recognize objects. The map tracks uncertain estimates and plans only through known free cells.

A decision can change without the world changing

The example selects work preparation under one explicit policy and talking with Papa under a relationship-prioritizing policy. Joy, peace, stimulation, and autonomy contribute alongside the existing five objective dimensions. The result is a proposed activity, not an automatically executed outcome.

Practice and rest have separate learning states

Guitar proficiency starts higher than voice or lyric recall. Quiet recall changes the lyric proxy without automatically improving guitar. Concentration raises rehearsal rate; incidental learning remains positive and a separate consolidation proxy grows during relaxation. These are proposed scenario rules for future testing, not fitted neuroscience.
