An observer outside the Person

The tracker owns its measurements. It has no command path into Alex’s Body or Will, so observation injects no disturbance in this scenario. His internal subjective map remains a distinct model. Session IDs, coordinate frames, timestamps, stale data, and missing lidar returns are explicit.

Keep the sessions distinct

The motion demonstration and sleep recording were not captured together. Source-relative time and bag receive time remain separate. The public source contains the adapters and synthetic tests; real study features, figures, manifests, and mixed-data bags stay in local study outputs.
