Why finite?

If each layer is bounded by Mα^d with 0 ≤ α < 1, the infinite tail is bounded by Mα^(D+1)/(1−α). A six-branch additive tree needs a sufficiently small per-branch gain for the total to remain summable. Those are assumptions to test, not evidence of infinite physical depth.

Read the evidence boundary

The supplied archive is a computational substrate. Its output demonstrates bounded state updates, hierarchy construction, and diagnostics under its own rules. It does not derive inverse-square laws, spectra, quantum mechanics, or a new physical law. Connecting it to physics still requires constrained compatibility assumptions, an emergent target, real data, held-out predictions, and comparisons against established models.

The Python and C++ implementations are conceptually aligned but are not bit-for-bit identical: defaults, random generators, and numerical details differ. Treat them as two reference surfaces until a cross-language fixture suite is added.
