MANNY GLOVER · OCTOBER 8, 2026 · A COMMUNITY VISION

Gay Georgia.

A place to arrive. A place to belong. A place to build a life.

A Georgia town renewed through welcome—especially inviting LGBTQ+ Palestinians, including Muslims, Christians, Jews, and secular humanists.

Many traditions. Shared dignity.

The dream is a flourishing community where queer people can live openly, neighbors can keep their faith or have no faith, and belonging does not demand that anyone erase their history. A refuge can also be a lively hometown: homes, gardens, small businesses, art, good food, and friendship.

Renewal with the people already there.

A welcoming town includes its existing residents. Revitalization should grow from willing partnerships, protect people from displacement, and make space for both newcomers and longtime neighbors. Palestinian welcome belongs alongside a broad invitation to LGBTQ+ people and allies.

From a vision to a proposal.

This is an aspirational statement, not an announcement of a purchase. The town has not yet been identified. Any practical proposal would begin with willing property owners and residents, a defined budget, and an achievable plan for housing and community life.
