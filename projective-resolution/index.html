The rule is explicit

project(X, Y, Z) = (f * X / Z, f * Y / Z)
cell(x, y) = (floor((x - offset) / epsilon), floor((y - offset) / epsilon))
A ~ B exactly when cell(project(A)) = cell(project(B))

The observed count is the number of occupied cells. A triangle can have 3 vertices and 1 observed class. One occupied cell alone does not reveal how many vertices it contains. This uses perspective projection of Euclidean objects; finite resolution supplies the grouping.

Projection coordinates use a fixed 800 x 290 image plane, f = 600. Display resizing scales the entire plane. Geometry remains visible as a reference overlay even when its observation cells merge. Face-on at equal distance, triangle sides and the broadside rod have equal projected lengths.
