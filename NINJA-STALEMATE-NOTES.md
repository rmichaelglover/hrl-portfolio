## Historical frame

`Enter the Dragon` is Bruce Lee's final completed feature. The American Film
Institute records Jackie Chan's uncredited appearance as a henchman. In Chan's
own recollection at an Academy event, Lee accidentally struck him with a staff
during a take, stopped when the take ended, embraced him, and checked on him.
The app therefore describes an on-set stunt accident and the concern afterward,
not a sparring match and not an exchange of near-full-contact punches.

“Ninja” describes the fictional chess atmosphere only. Bruce Lee and Jackie
Chan were Chinese/Hong Kong martial-arts film artists; the feature does not
recast them as Japanese ninja or write dialogue in their voices.
