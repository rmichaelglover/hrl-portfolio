Study notes & sources

“Stalemate Gambit” is our creative name for a survival theme, rather than an established opening claim. Composed teaching positions are identified in their comments. The historical game has two queens at its finish.

Our structure follows Lichess studies: chapter starting positions, move trees, comments, variations, and hidden lesson solutions. Downloaded PGN preserves those chess contents and colored-square annotations. Chapter practice settings must be set separately on Lichess; they do not transfer as PGN settings.
