You pushed 18.f5 and 19.g4, and Black helped clear the g-file. Your king wandered between f1 and f2 like a man searching for his car in a supermarket parking lot, while Black’s queen collected your rook and another pawn. An alarming amount of your furniture was leaving the premises.

But the surviving rook had a job: 25.Rxg4+. Black chose 25…Kh8, and you delivered 26.Qg7#—the queen protected by the rook on g4. Actual checkmate. Black won the shopping spree; you won the game. 🎉
