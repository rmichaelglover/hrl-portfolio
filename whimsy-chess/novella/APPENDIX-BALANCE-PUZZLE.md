# Appendix — Find the Force, Find the Misquote

This short quotation-identification puzzle is a transformative classroom exercise,
not a retelling of *Star Wars*. Three phrases belong to the screen tradition; one is
a famous altered recollection. Hunt for the lightly emphasized letters.

- **U**nderstand the position; **S**earch checks; **E**valuate replies.
  **T**hen **H**old **E**very candidate. **F**ind **O**pposition,
  **R**esources, **C**ounters, and **E**scapes, **L**uke.
- **N**otice the exact wording: **O**, **I** am your father.
- **T**here **H**ides **E**lsewhere **R**eserve; **E**ven **I**n
  **S**ilence **A**nother.
- **B**reathe **A**nd **L**ook **A**gain; **N**either **C**olor can ignore
  **E**very consequence.

The concealed lines read “USE THE FORCE LUKE,” “NO I AM YOUR FATHER,” “THERE IS
ANOTHER,” and “BALANCE.” The tempting phrase **“Luke, I am your father”** is the
misquotation. The chess lesson is exactness: familiar is not always accurate.
