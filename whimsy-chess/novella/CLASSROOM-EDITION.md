# The Woodland Losers — Classroom Edition

This is the complete novella’s five-session teaching route. Read the corresponding
section of `TWEEN-EDITION.md`, then load the named games in Maestro.

1. **Experiment with guardrails.** Kádas Cascade, Queen Dab, Kádas Stampede,
   Kádas Roundup, Carr Crash. Ask after every move: checks, captures, threats.
2. **Rules make creativity possible.** Schrödinger’s Flag, Horsey Surprise, both
   Crab games, Goldsmith Defense. Compare unusual moves with development principles.
3. **Progress over perfection.** Father’s Day Surprise, Kádas Houdini, We’re No
   Daisies, Dead on Move 2, Creepy Crawly Named. Replace “brilliant or bad” with a
   ladder: unsafe, playable, good, excellent.
4. **Finish and forgive.** One That Got Away, Stalemate Gambit, Enter the Dragon,
   Jerry Ball, Jerry Ballas. Practice basic mates and one sentence of good-sport
   language after every result.
5. **Build a repertoire.** Creepy Harriet, both Bongclouds, Crawling Crown, and
   Kádas Kakes. Compare
   the Italian, Ruy López, Queen’s Gambit, Sicilian, French, Caro–Kann, and
   Nimzo-Indian. End by annotating one move without an engine, then checking it.

Assessment is a reflection, not a rating: name one plan, one tactical detail, one
kind response to a loss, and one thing to practice next.
