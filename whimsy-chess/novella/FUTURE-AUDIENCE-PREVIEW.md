# A PG-Capped Goodbye to Future Readers

Someday the tween readers will become teenagers, voters, workers, parents, mentors,
and wonderfully seasoned chess enthusiasts. Some may discover prestige television,
including *The Queen’s Gambit*, and laugh at a future companion novella whose memes
are historically tidier than its fictional chess club.

That possible book belongs to a later shelf. It would still treat addiction,
competition, aging, and ambition with care rather than glamour; it would remain
free, ad-free, and welcoming. For now, close the clock gently, thank your opponent,
and carry the best lesson forward:

## Rare special-edition jacket copy

One hypothetical dust jacket may nod affectionately to Aunt Cindy and Uncle Gilbert,
whose spirited household review mixes family loyalty, regional rivalry, faith, and a
very loud “Roll Tide!” They need not approve of every fashionable television series.
If that removable jacket gets mysteriously lost, their concise review is:
**“Good riddance.”** The book beneath it remains courteous to artists and audiences.

**Growing older changes the questions. Being a good sport never goes out of style.**
