# The Woodland Losers — Advanced Companion

Keep the tween story; deepen the questions. For every one of Maestro’s 25 games:

- Identify the last position where the evaluation was still practically unclear.
- Give three candidate moves before calculating any one line.
- Separate a tactical error from a strategic concession.
- State what a proposed sacrifice buys. “Attack” is not precise enough.
- Compare engine truth with the decision a human could reasonably make on the clock.

## The Tal Laboratory

Mikhail Tal was Latvian and a Soviet representative—not non-Soviet—and became the
eighth World Champion in 1960. His reputation for wild attacking play makes him a
fine patron of disciplined imagination. Study a Tal combination by first hiding the
solution. Inventory forcing moves, calculate the quiet defensive resource, and only
then decide whether the sacrifice is sound or practically dangerous.

Apply that method to `5.Bxh6?` in Creepy Harriet and `22...Qh2+??` in Enter the
Dragon. A move does not inherit brilliance from its drama.

## Extra Appetizers

Add the Evergreen Game for coordination, the Opera Game for development, and a
classic Tal attack for calculation. These are study suggestions rather than invented
entries in Manny’s archive. The balance test is constant: initiative against
material, speed against verification, engine depth against human explanation.
