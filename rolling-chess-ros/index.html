“Forced” is a bounded adversarial strategy result, not a prediction that every player will choose it. No probability is assigned. Kings use check and checkmate, never capture. Variant repetition includes these extra rights; baseline draw signals are diagnostic only in variant mode.

Model: 80 cm wooden board; 12 mm radius solid metal sphere; fixed 1/240 s physics substeps; no-slip rolling acceleration (5/7)g along the slope; illustrative resistance and damped rail/maze collisions. Holes use an absorbing capture-well approximation. Board-actuator inertia, slipping, and high-speed rim impacts are not modeled. Rendered chess pieces are location markers, not physical colliders for the metal move token. Maze partitions and stranded metal balls are physical blockers. These can make a legal move difficult for this controller, including a knight jump; they do not change chess legality.

White-hole and black-hole language describes source and destination fixtures, not gravitational black holes. Entering a hole never lets a player discard their own piece. Castling, captures, en passant, king safety, and all four promotions remain governed by the chess engine. This test measures branch filtering from exact observations and candidate ordering from physical trials, not faster optimal chess search.
