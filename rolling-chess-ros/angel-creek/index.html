Water and motion

Flow sets downstream drift. Water temperature and viscosity set the scale of Brownian diffusion for a suspended 0.5 µm-radius tracer. The creek drawing is schematic. The signed Brownian step above is in micrometres.

Energy and evidence

The capacitor stores modeled harvest from 12 cm of hydraulic head. A pulse spends 50 mJ and opens a downstream observation on the next sample. The Angel uses that reading to update its trust in its three forecasters.

From replay to creek

This replay uses synthetic flow. The native ROS 2 node also accepts flow and temperature measured at 1 Hz, with an optional downstream reading for feedback. The garden, generator and circuit run as models.
