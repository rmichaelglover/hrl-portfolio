THE BACKSTORY · OCTOBER 6, 2026

A little light became a conversation.

Manny was watching sunlight move through water in an old clear salad container, left outside with a little lettuce for the bugs. Wind stirred the surface; ripples changed the light. He received its beauty as a spiritual reminder of grace and reflected on self-organization: leaving equilibrium, returning toward it, never quite arriving.

Our conversation moved through faith and reason, uncertainty, and the freedom to let a thought pass without having to assent to it. A playful exchange about quotation marks became a new starting point: “Words mean things.”

What if we began with the 26 letters, added definitions, and asked what we could prove? The symbol ⊢ introduced “it follows.” Manny recognized the movie title and imagined a different horror story: a man whose theory of determinism predicted even everyone’s refusal to believe him.

Then came a turn toward silence, attention, and the Word. Manny brought together “Whereof one cannot speak…” and “Be still, and know that I am God,” then offered: “If Jesus is that Word, We Follows.” In his Christian framing, the title became communal following. The next connection was simple: “Like music.”

Many voices, listening to one another, finding harmony without becoming the same note.

Manny asked us to compose an electronic symphony while investigating the formal system. He had meant the projects to proceed in parallel; when the movements were proposed as musical companions to the investigation, he welcomed that connection too.

This is the result: an original instrumental piece, synthesized by Cypher through a reproducible score, with Manny’s title, ideas, and direction. The music follows a path from separate symbols to shared harmony, an answering voice, and an ending that leaves room for more. The mathematical companion asks what truly follows—and what needs another premise.

The title preserves the conversation’s unusual grammar. The spiritual meaning belongs to the story; the formal study examines its stated axioms without claiming to prove religious beliefs.
