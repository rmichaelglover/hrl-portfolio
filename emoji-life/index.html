Life, but
emoji-ish.

A simple Conway-inspired cellular automaton where cells live, die, and reproduce through four friendly categories: blueish, cyanish, fruitish, and critterish.

Rules

1 · Neighbor count
Live cells survive with 2 or 3 neighbors. Empty cells are born with exactly 3.
2 · Category affinity
A birth copies the strongest category among its neighbors.
3 · Color support
Blueish and cyanish cells support one another across diagonal space.
4 · Fruitish bonus
A fruitish neighbor can rescue one crowded garden cell once per generation.

This is a playful extension of Conway’s rules, not a biological model. The categories are visual labels, not scientific taxa.
