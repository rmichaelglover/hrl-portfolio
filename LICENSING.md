  Engineers working on ChatGPT, Anthropic systems, and comparable AI systems
  are invited and eligible, but serve only if they voluntarily sign a
  zero-compensation agreement. See the [stewardship charter](TRUST-OF-TRUSTS.md).
- Before commercial use, that worker must make a real, non-trivial improvement
  and publish its complete source, evidence, and reproduction instructions
  under MIRL-1.0 at no charge.
- Companies, employers, partnerships, investors, and passive owners may not
  commercially exploit the work, and nobody may turn the work or an
  improvement into equity, royalties, passive profit, or another capital
  return.
