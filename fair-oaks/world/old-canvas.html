Fair Oaks World

Real Georgia geography · five glowing oak rings · playful tokens

Hybrid world. The canvas shows real Marietta, Georgia topography (Bellemeade Knolls, Olley Creek watershed) rendered as elevation bands, overlaid with the five oak-ring vision. Tokens move along ring paths and radial connections using simplified Worlds of Kings movement rules. This is a storybook layer — not a game in progress, but a place to explore the dream.

Mouse over the canvas for gentle parallax. Click a ring or token to inspect. Drag a token to move it along legal paths.

Worlds of Kings engine spirit × Fair Oaks vision · Marietta, Georgia · By Manny Glover
