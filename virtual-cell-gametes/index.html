The egg also contributes a rich cellular starting environment. A growing human needs much more than a DNA sequence: working cell machinery, changing signals, materials and energy.

FOR THE CURIOUS · OPEN RESEARCH NOTEBOOK

The picture is an illustration.
The accounting is executable.

This tour displays the saved output of our Python event model. It begins with a euploid MII oocyte and fusion-ready sperm at the membrane. It checks event ordering, sperm identity, a supplied effective block, meiosis-II completion and nuclear DNA accounting. Its normal endpoint is two pronuclei before DNA replication; it does not depict their fusion into one nucleus.

The diagram adds educational organelles and a schematic activation glow. Their positions, counts, motions and appearance are illustrative. They are not outputs of a molecular simulation. A two-sperm branch flags polyspermy and stops after the supplied fusions; it does not predict embryo development.

Biological sources & limits

Source context: meiosis and fertilization, human sperm CatSper electrophysiology, human IZUMO1–JUNO recognition structures, and PLCζ-induced calcium responses in human oocytes. Recognition and fusion are distinct steps. Human microinjection experiments do not establish calibrated natural-fertilization dynamics.

No fitted kinetics, event probabilities, ATP budget, anatomical geometry or first-cleavage prediction is implemented. Organelles here are parts of a cell, not extra cells in a lineage. Research assumptions and species provenance are recorded in the roadmap.
