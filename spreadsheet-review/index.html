A review, not a rewrite

This pilot checks saved errors, broken references, and vertical formula patterns with four agreeing neighbors.

It does not recalculate Excel, verify prices, inspect charts or Power Query, or change your workbook.

A fixed amount, a return, or a subtotal can be intentional. Treat pattern flags as questions.
