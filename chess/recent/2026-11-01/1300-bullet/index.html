THREE PATTERNS TO CARRY FORWARD

King safety before the next attack.

  1. Before advancing pawns in front of your king, scan the queen and bishop diagonals to f7 and h7.
  2. After a queen-for-material exchange, immediately inspect passed pawns and promotion races.
  3. Before sending the king after a rook, count every enemy pawn already near promotion.
