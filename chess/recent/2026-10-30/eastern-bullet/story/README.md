# Dilorias Takes Her Bow

A separate storybook companion to the existing Eastern Bullet Arena review.
The earlier review, game data, commentary, source PGN and aesthetic are preserved.

The new page puts three large story illustrations and a three-scene fictional
narrative first. Its cyan, magenta, yellow and black palette uses dark readable
text on warm paper and bright accent blocks. Exact moves are linked as optional
replay receipts rather than embedding the analytical interface.

The narrative's main chess action follows the final exported game:

- 4...Qxf3 5.exf3: Dilorias gives herself for the knight on f3.
- 16...Rxc3+ 17.bxc3: Castle Andora takes White's queen, then a pawn takes the rook.
- 38.f8=Q+: the pawn that captured Dilorias on f3 becomes White’s new queen.
- 46.Qg2#: the recorded game ends with White winning.

The knight fork discussed at the table is the unplayed ...Nxf3+ from game 12.
The cast dialogue and illustrated theatre are fictional; they do not change the
recorded moves. A reachable physical whiteboard remains part of the staged room.

Exactly three full-size story illustrations are saved under `art/`. They were
created with the built-in image generation tool. `art/prompts.json` records the
complete prompts, saved asset names and provenance. The artwork is symbolic,
not a chess diagram or a portrait of real people.

This is a buildless HTML/CSS page. Run a local HTTP server to preview it.
There is no replay script, player tracking, animation or external font dependency.
