What to learn without draining the fun

Queen raids can win material, but only when the queen has an exit. Sacrifices need a concrete continuation, especially near the king. When an opponent attacks your queen or threatens mate, look for forcing moves first: checks, captures, and direct threats. Here, both players repeatedly found the entertaining move before the cleanest move—and that is exactly why the game tells such a good story.
