Let it be written. Let it be checked. Let the correction be welcomed.

Proof, perspective and faith meet in an inquiry into what we can establish, what remains open, and when we know enough to act.

Knowledge, its foundations, and its limits

The manuscript develops the Manuelian distinction between known knowns, known unknowns, unknown knowns and unknown unknowns. It gives explicit operators for discovery and resolution, demonstrating how their order changes the resulting knowledge state.

Twenty-four chapters connect axioms and conditional proof, the liar paradox, Gödel’s theorems, Cantor’s construction, the continuum hypothesis, probability transformations, chess, origins and Christian interpretation. Mathematical results, philosophical arguments and theological readings retain their respective standards of justification.

Six figures accompany the discussion. The source bundle includes the editable manuscript, LaTeX, vector figures, plotting scripts, verification results and a file integrity manifest.

Independent manuscript prepared for author review. Figures show mathematical constructions and conceptual models.
