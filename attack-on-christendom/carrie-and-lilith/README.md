# Carrie & Lilith

A reading room alongside the journal’s existing *Mary Comes to Roost* material.

- `essay.md`: nonfiction criticism and personal theology developed from Michael
  Emanuel Glover and Cypher’s conversation, with source notes.
- `story.md`: *The Road Beyond the Fire*, an original six-chapter unofficial
  alternate-universe fanfiction. It has its own continuity and does not modify
  the earlier story or its preserved drafts.
- `index.html`, `essay.html`, `story.html`: static public reading editions.
- `reading.css`: responsive reading and print styles, alongside `../journal.css`.

Regenerate with `python3 attack-on-christendom/carrie-and-lilith/build.py` from
the repository root. Requires the already-installed Python `markdown` package.
No JavaScript, remote fonts, autoplay, or account flow is required to read.

This is independent fan work. Stephen King’s characters and the adaptation’s
characters retain their respective underlying rights. The site’s license does
not grant ownership of those characters. The Lilith genealogy and earthly-
divinity premise are this story’s inventions, not claims about official canon
or shared religious doctrine. Public prose follows `CONTENT-RATING.md`.
