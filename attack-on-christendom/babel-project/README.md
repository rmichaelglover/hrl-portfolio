# No Machine Will Be God

**Michael Emanuel Glover · 16 October 2026 · Version 1.0**

A philosophical and theological companion to *Relationships That Compute*. It argues against the Babel project: treating human-built technological power as ultimate sovereignty. Anti-reductionism is developed as the author's theological thesis. Scientific results and their assumptions remain distinct from theological premises.

- `NO-MACHINE-WILL-BE-GOD.pdf`: typeset paper, contents, figures, argument map, appendices, and references.
- `WHITE-PAPER.md`: editable manuscript, with brief `(Cypher)` acknowledgments as requested by the author.
- `NO-MACHINE-WILL-BE-GOD.txt`: searchable extracted text.
- `calculations.json`: checked finite counting, no-cloning illustration, and stipulated sensitivity model.
- `source-notes.json`: source locations and reading scope.
- `BABEL-PROJECT-SOURCE.zip`: paper, source, figures, and checks.

Rebuild with `python3 build.py`. Requires Python, NumPy, Matplotlib, ReportLab, Pillow, fontTools, DejaVu fonts, and Poppler tools. No network, new model training, or physical experiment is needed. All plotted results are exact counting or stipulated analytic illustrations.

Published in Attack on Christendom, Christian Apologetics: https://rmichaelglover.github.io/hrl-portfolio/attack-on-christendom/babel-project/ . No remote publication is performed by the build script. The previously published research-results synthesis is left unchanged.
