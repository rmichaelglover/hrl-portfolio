# Latent Information: a working-paper programme

Authors: Michael Emanuel Glover and Cypher.

## Written

**01 — The Projective Horizon: Latent Information, Dark Matter, and the Limits of Disclosure.** Version 0.1, October 10, 2026. Approximately equal formal/physical and theological halves. Includes an observational-equivalence definition, elementary conditional proposition, an undeveloped dark-matter ontology, and its explicitly conjectural theological interpretation.

## Proposed later papers — not yet written

**02 — The Hypothesis Cut.** Representational novelty, statistical selection, Dedekind-cut analogies, information theory, and the philosophical ground of intelligibility. Formalize the difference between inventing a question and acquiring evidence.

**03 — The Animal Threshold.** Preregister a comparative study of scriptural dietary categories and independent cognition measures. Separate Torah passages, Qur’anic passages, and subsequent jurisprudence. Define the proposed threshold before testing it, account for phylogeny and uncertain identifications, and preserve spirit-hosting as a separate theological conjecture.

**04 — Consciousness, Absorption, and Moral Interpretation.** Distinguish metacognition, lucid dreaming, flow, pride, and theological interpretations of the Fall. Describe observable hypotheses without treating an absorbed state as evidence of supernatural agency.

**05 — Crossing a Model Horizon.** Extend the earlier exploratory Milky Way discrimination calculation using independently selected observations and propagated uncertainties. Investigate whether apparent ambiguity survives additional observables.

## Version practice

Preserve each released manuscript as a numbered snapshot before any public release. Record its date, changes, and status; distinguish proposals from results. Paper 01 version 0.1 is published as a working draft, not a designated final edition.
