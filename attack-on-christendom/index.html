FROM THE EDITOR

Can I also let someone go to sleep?

This journal examines what happens when the language of goodness meets ordinary conduct. Its title announces a criticism of complacency and performed virtue, through argument and satire. The narrator stands inside the examination.

A blessing can ask for another person’s good without claiming their attention. A joke can offer company. A conversation can finish kindly. These are small acts, and they are suitable material for a front page.
