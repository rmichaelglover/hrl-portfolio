The Floating Guitar

From Marietta’s leafy streets to Panama City’s evening lights. A walking-and-snorkeling daydream, with a protective wetsuit, snorkeling gear, and a guitar that carries the song afloat.

The first verse begins beneath the Georgia trees. Footsteps keep the rhythm. The guitar comes along, waiting for the water.

In the daydream, the road opens into a shoreline. Walking gives way to snorkeling; sunlight dances across the reef. Above the traveler, the guitar bobs in its floating cradle, keeping its little place in the sky.

By evening, Panama City glows across the water. There is one more song to play before the stars arrive.

Every shoreline gets a verse.

Original imaginative concept art created with OpenAI’s image generation tool. This is a storybook vision, not a navigational route or expedition plan.
