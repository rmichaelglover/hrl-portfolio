# Your Valentine, for Now and Forever

## Chapter XX · The Names

*A fictional Halloween romance. Tara and Emanuel are theatrical characters; this scene is an imagined story, not a claim about real people or events.*

The cemetery gates sighed shut behind them.

Tara was twenty-six, dressed in a battered black coat with silver moons painted beneath her eyes. Emanuel, twenty-eight, wore a cracked porcelain mask with a stitched smile. It was frightening enough to make the crows complain, but when he saw her looking, he lifted it carefully away.

**EMANUEL:** Too much?

**TARA:** Only if you’re planning to bite.

**EMANUEL:** I was thinking of offering you half my chocolate.

**TARA:** Then you’re safe.

They laughed, startling two crows from a yew tree. Beyond the iron fence, the city glowed orange with Halloween lanterns. Here, old stones held the quiet. They walked between them slowly, trading stories: Tara’s first terrible ghost costume; Emanuel’s grandmother teaching him to cook by smell instead of recipe; the songs they each pretended not to love.

The laughter softened into silences that felt comfortable rather than empty.

At a stone bench, Emanuel turned the mask in his hands. Without it, he looked tired and kind.

**TARA:** What’s the thing you wish people understood about you?

He took a moment.

**EMANUEL:** That I’m not always brave just because I show up.

She nodded. It was her turn to search for the right words.

**TARA:** I wish people knew that being bright doesn’t mean I’m never lonely.

He offered her the chocolate. She broke it in two and gave him the larger piece.

**EMANUEL:** An act of great generosity.

**TARA:** Don’t tell anyone. I have a reputation.

Wind moved through the branches. Somewhere outside the cemetery, children shouted “Trick or treat!” and hurried onward, their footsteps fading down the block.

Emanuel reached for her hand, pausing just long enough for her to choose. Tara took it.

**EMANUEL:** Your Valentine, for now and forever?

She leaned her shoulder against his.

**TARA:** For now. We’ll make forever one kind day at a time.

They stayed a little longer, sharing what mattered and leaving the rest for another night.

## Years Later · The Reveal

Every Halloween, they returned to the old cemetery—not because they were haunted, they said, but because the lanterns made the path look like a bridge between worlds.

The years had changed little things first. Emanuel no longer needed the cracked porcelain mask to make Tara laugh. Tara had added silver stars to her moonlit makeup. They brought better chocolate now, though they still broke it in half and argued over who got the larger piece.

They remembered that first night: two strangers on a cold stone bench, trading stories beneath the crows. Back then, they offered each other one brave sentence at a time. Now their conversations wandered freely—from songs and family legends to the small kindnesses that keep a life stitched together.

**TARA:** You know, I think we’ve been introducing ourselves wrong.

Emanuel turned toward her.

**EMANUEL:** Oh?

She took his hand.

**TARA:** I’m Tara.

**EMANUEL:** And I’m Emanuel.

The names settled between them, warm as lantern light. The spooky strangers had become familiar; the familiar had become beloved. Beyond the gate, children’s laughter drifted through the autumn night.

**EMANUEL:** Your Valentine?

**TARA:** For now—and forever, one kind day at a time.

The crows lifted into the moonlit branches. They stayed on the bench, sharing chocolate, stories, and the lovely information of who they had been all along.
