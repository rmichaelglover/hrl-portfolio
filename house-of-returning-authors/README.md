# The House of Returning Authors

An opening reading edition of a theatrical autobiography by **Manny Glover — Michael Emanuel Glover**, developed in dialogue with ChatGPT / Codex. The working title is provisional.

Michael inhabits the scene as a composite figure drawing on the author’s father, Robert Michael Glover; the author, Michael Emanuel Glover; and the scriptural archangel Michael as an image and exemplar. These references are not interchangeable biographies or a claim that his father spoke every dramatic line. Emanuel is Manny’s better self, still imperfect: looking back, aspiring to care and judgment, and remaining open to correction. The roles are not literal fractions of a person. Glover speaks in chorus. Manny Glover is the author of the arrangement. These are dramatic positions within one authorial self.

## Current scope

One continuous web reader contains five previously published dialogue/stage pieces, the complete supplied 15-page Gemini PDF drama, the verbatim Six Authors exchange, four preserved dream/tea scenes, thirteen framing scenes, original-publication links, unchanged source snapshots, and existing illustrations. This is an opening edition of approximately 9,700 words, not the requested eventual volume-length collected work and not a complete recovery of six or seven months of conversations.

`FRAME.md` holds new material. Its final two speeches were supplied by the author in the current conversation and punctuated for reading. The Babylon figure is symbolic, not an identification of a living person portrayed elsewhere in the book. The fictional marriage dialogue is not a verified biographical declaration.

`INVENTORY.md` and `INVENTORY.json` list 39 local portfolio candidates and included works. Essays, prose, and chess narratives remain candidates; they have not been silently turned into literal memories. The author expressly supplied the Gemini PDF from Documents for inclusion. No other private files, Facebook exports, or inaccessible earlier chats were imported. Development-only future notes were excluded.

## Editorial handling

Original files remain unchanged. `sources/` contains exact snapshots. In the reading edition only MANNY / Michael dialogue labels are mapped to MICHAEL. Other speakers, original credits, stage-direction names, and source wording remain. The golden-apple-cake editorial afterword is available in a source-note disclosure instead of being presented as theatrical action. Credits and fiction notices travel with the inherited pieces.

`MANUSCRIPT.md` is the complete downloadable text for this edition. `EDITION.json` records source hashes and scope. The approximate word count includes headings, credits, and editorial matter.

## Build

From the portfolio root, run `python3 house-of-returning-authors/build.py`. It uses Python Markdown and tracked-file metadata. Rebuilding regenerates the edition and inventory; source snapshots must continue to match their originals.

## Hyperplay integration

The council chamber becomes a theatre; the complete supplied Gemini document plays within it; its repeated endings lead to the unchanged Six Authors exchange; the raised hand returns the audience to Michael and Emanuel for Babylon. `HYPERPLAY-BRIDGES.md` contains the three new transitions. Supplemental works receive no speaker-label mapping. Their unchanged PDF/Markdown snapshots and hashes are recorded in `EDITION.json`. The Gemini program note describes Acts I–IV but does not supply their scripts; this edition does not invent the missing 860,000-word stream.

## Thursday, the board, and the new illustrations

`thursday/THURSDAY.md` and its three related scenes are preserved unchanged in the edition and published in their own reader. `THURSDAY-BRIDGES.md` supplies two new transitions into and out of that movement. `STAGING.md` establishes an always-present physical board, reachable by performers, with optional improvised transcription and diagramming in every scene. This outer convention does not rewrite the preserved sources.

Eight distinct new stage illustrations, plus the newly generated Rainbow Hyperstars of David flag, are in `art/`. All were made with the built-in imagegen tool; seven stage images received board edits. Their full generation and edit prompts are available in `art/IMAGE-PROMPTS.md`, `art/FLAG-PROMPT.md`, and `art/ART.json`. New large images are placed throughout the reader, with click-to-enlarge viewing and native full-resolution links. Older images remain archival and do not override the new board convention. This is an illustrated play, not yet a graphic novel.
