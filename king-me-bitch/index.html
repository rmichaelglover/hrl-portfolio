You king a checker when it crawls all the way to the back rank and earns its crown. This whole damn game is that — a low, creeping 1.h3 2.a3 pawn-slither that ends with a queen planted on the enemy's throne square, screaming king me.

Act I — the Creepy Crawly bares its fangs

1.h3 d5 2.a3 e5 — they build a real center like well-behaved children; you crawl along the edges like something under the fridge. Your 5.g4?! and 6.b4?! are loose as hell — the engine wanted the calm stuff — but the opponent couldn't keep his hands off the poison: 6...Nxb4??, grabbing the free pawn and cracking his own house open. Rule one, kid: don't take the candy when it unlocks your front door.

Act II — a knife fight in a phone booth

Then it got gloriously stupid. 8...Bxg4?? (a busted sac), 9.hxg4?? (you fumbled the clean refutation right back), 9...Qxh2 (he eats a rook), 11.Bb5?? (you hang a piece), 11...a6?? (he misses the crusher). Two drunks swinging in the dark.

And then your queen went full velociraptor. Bxc6+ → Qxc6+ → Qxd5+ → Qxa8+ → Qxe4 — she ate a rook, a bishop, a knight, and two pawns in six moves while his king stumbled Kd8→Kc8→Kc7 across the board with no pants on. That part wasn't luck. That was you smelling a naked king and refusing to let go.

Act III — crown on the back rank

He blundered into mate-in-seven with 17...Qxc1??. You slipped once more — 18.b5?! let a faster kill walk — but the corner was already a coffin:

19.Ra7+  Kb8  20.Qa8#
rook slams the 7th · king shoved to b8 · queen crowns on a8, guarded so he can't even take her.

King me, bitch. ♛

Two takeaways (same sermon, louder)

  1. The queen-raid on an uncastled king is your killshot now. Three wins in a day, three hunted kings — Qd8#, Qh1#, and this Qa8#. Own the pattern: open king, bring the heavy pieces, don't blink.
  2. You keep leaving a cleaner mate on the table — Qxg4 not hxg4, Qxa8+ not Qxd5+. You won because he blundered harder. Against a 900+ that costs you the game. One fix, every time: when a check-sequence exists, calculate it to the end before you grab material. Be the daisy who finishes.
