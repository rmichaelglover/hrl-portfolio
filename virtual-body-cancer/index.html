Where your HRL algorithm enters

The unchanged sparse HRL engine labels a separate 120-cell synthetic patch benchmark. Accuracy: priors alone 55.8%; pairwise factors 68.3%; pairwise plus three-way factors 70.0%. All three converged. A separate unknown-label demonstration rejected 12 cells. Compatibility is correct by construction in this toy dataset; these results are not biological validation or calibrated confidence.

The inference and dynamical components remain separate. A measured observation model, cell-state-to-rate mapping and external validation are required to connect them responsibly.

Drive audit: measured data and new calculations

The expanded accessible-storage inventory covered 880,934 files; 45,884 selected text/code files were searched. It located the existing HRL2026 virtual-cell code and a cached dataset of 5,310 measured CRISPR assay ranks. Coverage and exclusions are recorded in the full audit report; private raw inventory and physiology records are excluded from this publication.

New sequence-disjoint and gene-disjoint evaluations use existing sequence features and sparse HRL. The original row-based split shared 271 distinct test sequences with training. All ten new HRL fits converged; their small point-estimate gains do not establish significant superiority.

Benchmark results and provenance

An isolated virtual-cell snapshot fixes the original missing-method crash, entropy normalization, state-change reporting and abstention coding. A seven-node, three-level hierarchy passed ten recursive steps. These remain categorical computations, not physical human-cell simulations. See execution checks.

Can this synthetic model sustain tumor-free stability?

A new eigenvalue analysis finds that the nominal resistant-population stability threshold requires constant dimensionless exposure above 5.181195, while maintaining the assumed reserve floor of 0.7 allows at most 0.136364. The feasible intersection is empty at the nominal settings and in all 24 arbitrary parameter configurations.

This is a local-stability result for constant exposure in the implemented model. It does not establish the behavior of arbitrary schedules, actual cancer biology or patients. Full stability calculations.
