    🌿 Field notes: where these neighbors belong

    This is an original, fictional friendship world. Named wildlife uses broad country and geographic-range sketches, coastal hints, and rough habitat filters. These are illustrative distributions, not verified sightings, abundance estimates, or exact habitat maps. Unresearched regions use explicitly labeled animal groups; some quiet places have no character.

    Night brings different neighbors. Koalas belong to the eastern mainland Australia sketch; kiwi neighbors stay in the main New Zealand sketch; penguins use southern coastal anchors. Invited friends are story visitors, separate from native map residents. Your chosen avatar is a traveler, not a distribution claim.

    Geography: Natural Earth v5.1.2. Countries, territories, and mapped disputed areas share this world; map coverage is not a sovereignty count. Gardens and gathering materials are imagined. Model, sources, and design notes ↗
