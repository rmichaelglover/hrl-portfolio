# Jihad Incorporated: A Holy Playlist

## A unification programme of Abrahamic faiths

This is a cross-genre listening draft. Every selection is devotional, prayerful, scriptural, or explicitly praise-oriented within an Abrahamic tradition. Jewish, Christian, Islamic, Baháʼí, and Rastafari selections are labeled rather than blended into one undifferentiated theology.

## Editorial principle

The playlist is unified by devotional intention and ethical aspiration, not by pretending that the traditions say the same things. The sequence moves from Jewish roots through Christian praise, Islamic remembrance, Baháʼí universalism, and Rastafari scripture-conscious reggae, then returns to contemporary worship.

## YouTube transfer

Open each search link, select the official or rights-holder upload where available, and add it to a new public playlist titled:

**Jihad Incorporated: A Holy Playlist — A Unification Programme of Abrahamic Faiths**

“Jihad” is used here in the broad sense of disciplined striving; the playlist is explicitly nonviolent, devotional, educational, and interfaith.
