How the simulation works

Ants choose local routes using distance, fading trail signals, and a preference against immediate backtracking. Returning ants reinforce successful journeys. Congestion slows travel. This is a simplified game model, not a validated account of any ant species or evidence of biological computing.

Deliveries earn 3 credits. Every mission grants 30 bonus credits and a larger colony. There is no time limit. Closing a route lets ants already on it finish crossing.
