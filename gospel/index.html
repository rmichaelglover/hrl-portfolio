The Gospel is the good news that God does not abandon us. In Jesus Christ, God meets human sin and sorrow with mercy, offers forgiveness, and opens a living hope through the cross and resurrection.

THE HEART OF THE MESSAGE

Grace is an invitation.

1 · God loves

Every person bears dignity and is invited into God's love. We begin with welcome, not fear.

2 · We need mercy

Sin is the real damage we do and carry: broken trust, injustice, pride, harm, and distance from God. We do not heal by pretending it is absent.

3 · Jesus gives himself

Christ enters our condition, dies, and rises. The cross shows costly love; the resurrection announces that death and despair do not get the last word.

4 · Receive and follow

Faith is a response to grace: turn toward God, trust Christ, receive forgiveness, and practice love with a changed life.

A QUIET RESPONSE

You can speak plainly.

There is no magic formula and no performance test. If you want to begin, you might pray:

“Jesus Christ, I need your mercy. Thank you for loving me and giving yourself for us. Forgive my sins, teach me to trust you, and help me love God and my neighbor. Lead me into truth, courage, and peace. Amen.”

Then take one honest next step: read one of the Gospels, speak with a trusted Christian community, make amends where you can, and practice mercy. Questions are welcome. Doubt can be brought into the light.
