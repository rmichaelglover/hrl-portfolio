How to play

Free play: improvise with eight pentatonic notes. The octave repeats give your melodies room to wander.

Echo challenge: press Listen, watch and hear a short melody, then repeat it. A missed note restarts your attempt gently. Complete five rounds, from three notes to seven.

Every newly explored note earns 2 XP. Each correct challenge note earns 5 XP once per position per round; completed rounds earn 20 bonus XP. Replaying completed notes does not farm XP.

Nature behind the instrument

Real spiders can sense vibrations through their webs. This musical instrument uses an artistic sound mapping and decorative animation; it does not reproduce a species’ web mechanics, hearing, or intelligence.
