  • An Axiomatic and Numerical Approach to Modeling Bipolar Disorder · PDF

    A four-dimensional Gaussian-mixture proposal with projective geometry, formal proofs and synthetic calculations. It is an unvalidated research model, not a diagnostic or treatment tool.

  • Magnum Economicus: Computation Munster · PDF

    A mathematical inquiry into prosperity, with measured World Bank GDP and Gini series for six countries and separately labeled synthetic allocation scenarios.

  • Drugs Are Good: An Allopath’s Journey Through Medicine, Healing, and Danger · PDF

    A first-person inquiry into medicine, evidence, exposure, benefit and danger. Numerical compounds and responses are fictional and dimensionless; no clinical treatment is recommended.
