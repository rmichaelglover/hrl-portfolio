Where did this position come from?

A tiny variant. Ordinary chess rules.

The only extra rule is where we start: a verified position from a real game with exactly one legal move. After that move, we continue only if the next player also has exactly one legal move. Otherwise, this little exercise is complete.

These positions can come from difficult games. Finding the legal move is the whole task; the game’s result does not determine whether you did well here.
