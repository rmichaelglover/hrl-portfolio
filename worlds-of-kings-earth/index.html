      A MAP IS A MODEL. ITS SOURCES SHOULD BE VISIBLE.

      Geography first. Rules we can inspect.

      Borders remain borders.

      Actual province polygons are simplified for display; they are not replaced by squares. Ocean areas follow a twelve-degree latitude–longitude grid, clipped against mapped land. Coast-separated parts remain separate spaces.

      A compass, not a hidden guess.

      Candidate cardinal routes follow border connections. Diagonal candidates use corner contacts or two border steps. Click a region to inspect the choices. Coastal gaps, small-country deployment, and polar conventions must be validated before these become full-match rules.

      The original stays yours.

      The fictional three-country game remains a playable, simpler edition and a rules reference. This separate atlas adds real geography without changing that game’s board, engine, or saved matches.

      Made with Natural Earth, pinned to release v5.1.2. Its map includes countries, territories, and disputed areas and generally uses de facto boundaries. The displayed count is map coverage, not a count of universally recognized sovereign states. Population figures are source estimates, not live census data.

      The atlas and your journey are stored or processed in this browser. No online country reservation or multiplayer service is connected. Design notes and next milestones · Source manifest · Preserved prototype reference.
