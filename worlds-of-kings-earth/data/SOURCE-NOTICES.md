# Geographic source notices

Made with Natural Earth: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

Pinned source repository release: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2

The country, province, and populated-place map data are public domain under Natural Earth’s terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Source attribution and limitations are retained even though credit is not required. Raw download hashes and exact URLs are in `manifest.json`. Derived geometry is simplified for cartographic display. Province adjacency and movement candidates are this project’s experimental derivations, not rules supplied or endorsed by Natural Earth.

Countries, dependencies, territories, and disputed areas follow the pinned Natural Earth dataset. Its default de facto map and labels do not constitute a political recognition claim. Administrative levels differ among countries. Names and population estimates may be historical or approximate.

The map data are distinct from the portfolio’s code and writing licenses. The bundled D3 and d3-geo-projection renderers retain their separate licenses under `vendor/`.
