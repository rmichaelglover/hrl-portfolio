# Education Pathways

An application-planning workspace for Michael “Manny” Glover’s next degree: a
research-led transition from computer vision, economics, and constraint-based AI
into civil/environmental engineering, hydrology, infrastructure intelligence,
and computational social science.

## Current recommendation

Apply primarily to **funded PhD programs in civil/environmental engineering**.
Frame the research around trustworthy sensing and computational models for small
watersheds, stormwater systems, rail and urban infrastructure. Keep a small set
of computational-social-science programs as interdisciplinary alternatives. Use
a technical MS only where it supplies missing engineering prerequisites at a
reasonable net cost. Treat a JD or another self-funded degree as a later option,
not the default next move.

The strongest geographic clusters are:

1. **Atlanta / Georgia:** Georgia Tech CEE and adjacent public-policy or computing
   collaborators; the most direct continuity with prior degrees and local work.
2. **Sunnyvale–San José–Santa Clara County:** Stanford CEE/Sustainable Urban
   Systems for the research PhD; San José State and Santa Clara University for
   applied MS pathways. This is also Manny’s technically true hometown region.
3. **Yosemite / Sierra gateways:** UC Merced Environmental Systems for
   place-based watershed research; UC Davis CEE or Hydrologic Sciences for the
   deepest conventional hydrology doctorate; Fresno State MSCE-WREE for an
   applied southern-gateway option.
4. **Additional comparison regions:** Chicago, Indianapolis, Austin,
   Raleigh–Durham, New York, Los Angeles/north SoCal, San Diego, Seoul,
   Southeast Asia, and Skopelos/Greece. These remain a research queue until a
   faculty-level fit and current cost are verified.

## Privacy and evidence rules

This public copy contains no home street address, disability/medical details,
private correspondence, or unsupported claim that anyone has agreed to write a
letter. Candidate recommenders are described by relationship and evidence until
Manny confirms names and permission. Every cost carries a year and source;
unknowns stay unknown until an official offer arrives.
