## Selected research and technical contributions

- Developed hierarchical relaxation-labeling software supporting sparse,
  arbitrary-order compatibility constraints, priors, and explicit noise labels.
- Demonstrated order-three factors resolving molecular chirality on 231/231 test
  cases where pairwise compatibility cannot distinguish handedness; reproducible
  code and interactive demonstration published in the portfolio.
- Built a 1,600-line constraint-solving engine identifying brake components in
  wayside railcar imagery and emitting inspection flags at Beena Vision Systems.
- Built and licensed a C++ CT fiducial-registration pipeline validated to
  approximately 0.5 mm for image-guided cryotherapy.
- Invented first-frame optical-marker identification at Motion Reality, raising
  accuracy from approximately 50% to 98% in a 60 Hz multi-camera system.
