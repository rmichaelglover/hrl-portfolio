What makes this HRL

Objects are contexts. Labels are candidate continuations. Empirical frequency supplies a persistent prior. Shared motifs and suffix contexts supply compatibility. A multiplicative, normalized update repeats until the field settles. The noise label is first-class, and hierarchical backoff lets a coarse memory speak when a fine one has never seen the position.
