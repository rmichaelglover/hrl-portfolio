# Public content standard

The GitHub Pages site is intended for tweens, families, classrooms, and adult
researchers. Public pages target a broad **PG** standard without reducing the
technical level: doctoral mathematics, computer science, physics, philosophy,
history, and literature remain welcome.

Public pages should avoid explicit sexual material, graphic injury, profanity,
glorified substance use, hate or dehumanization, and adult-only interactive
experiences. Brief non-graphic references to conflict, death, difficult history,
religion, or health may appear when contextually necessary and educational.

Older experiments outside this standard may remain visible in Git history for
scholarly provenance, but their adult material should not be served by GitHub
Pages. A neutral archive notice may occupy a former URL.

This is an editorial audience standard, not an assertion of an official film
rating and not a substitute for a teacher's or caregiver's judgment.
